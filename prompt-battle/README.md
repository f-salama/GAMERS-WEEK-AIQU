# Prompt Battle

**A 1v1 AI game where two players recreate the same picture using only words.**

**Play / Demo:** [aiqu-prompt-battle.vercel.app](https://aiqu-prompt-battle.vercel.app)

---

## About

Prompt Battle is a quick, fun, head-to-head game. A target image appears on the big screen, and each player has 60 seconds to describe it as precisely as they can in a single prompt. The AI turns both prompts into images, an AI judge scores each one against the target, and the closest match wins.

It's a website: **no app and no install**. Anyone can join, gamers or not!

## How to Play

1. **Target image:** When the round begins, a target image appears on the big screen.
2. **Join:** Each player scans their side's **QR code** with their phone, types their name, and taps **Ready**.
3. **Start:** The organizer presses **Start**.
4. **Write your prompt:** Each player has **60 seconds** to write **one prompt** describing the image as precisely as possible, in **Arabic or English**.
5. **Generate:** The AI turns both prompts into images.
6. **Judge:** An AI judge compares each image to the target and gives it a **match score**.
7. **Reveal:** After a drum-roll reveal, the player whose image is closest wins, and their name appears on the big screen.

## Features

- 1v1 rounds that take about a minute
- Play from your phone by scanning a QR code
- Prompts in **Arabic or English**
- AI image generation and AI judging
- Drum-roll reveal with the winner's name on the big screen
- No app to download, just open the website
